Career Resources

Practical guidance to prepare for campus placements and early-career interviews.

Resume & Cover Letter

  • Keep your resume to one page and lead with measurable impact (projects, internships, metrics).
  • List skills that match the job description; put relevant tech and coursework near the top.
  • Proofread carefully — typos signal poor attention to detail.
  • Tailor a short cover letter only when the company asks for one or the role is highly competitive.

Aptitude Round Strategies

  • Practice time-boxed sets (15–20 questions) to build speed without sacrificing accuracy.
  • Master ratios, percentages, profit & loss, time & work, and permutations first.
  • Skip and return: mark tough questions and finish easier ones first.
  • Review explanations after each practice session — patterns repeat across company tests.

Technical Interview Checklist

  • Know core DSA: arrays, strings, hashing, trees, graphs, and common sorting algorithms.
  • Be ready to explain time and space complexity for every solution you write.
  • Prepare 2–3 projects you can discuss end-to-end (problem, design, trade-offs, results).
  • Brush up on language fundamentals (OOP, memory, concurrency basics for your stack).

GD & HR Round Guidance

  • In group discussions, listen actively, add structured points, and avoid interrupting.
  • Prepare stories for teamwork, conflict, failure, and leadership using the STAR format.
  • Know the company: products, recent news, and why you want to join.
  • Ask thoughtful questions about the team, role expectations, and growth path.
